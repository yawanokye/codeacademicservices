Student Support Services

Tell us what you need help with

Use this form for a complaint or a service request. You will receive a permanent reference number and can track the matter with your email address.

1. Matter type and routing

Select the option that best describes the matter. Student Support Services may reclassify it during screening.

2. Your details

This portal supports undergraduate, postgraduate, certificate and other CoDE learners. These details allow the responsible unit to contact you and protect ticket access.

3. Describe the matter and attach evidence

Do not include passwords or payment card information. Evidence is visible only to Student Support and the office formally assigned to your matter.

Up to 10 files, maximum 15 MB each. Include only evidence relevant to this matter.

Track an existing ticket

Enter the reference from your acknowledgement and the same email address used when submitting.

What happens next?

  1. Student Support Services acknowledges and screens the matter.
  2. The ticket is routed to the responsible unit with a target response time.
  3. You receive updates through the portal and may reopen an unresolved matter.

Urgent or sensitive matters receive a priority pathway and restricted handling.
