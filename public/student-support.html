Student Support Services

How can we help?

Submit a complaint or request a service. You will receive a permanent reference and can follow every update to resolution.

1. Choose the service

Choose the closest category. Student Support Services will confirm the classification and responsible unit.

Student Support Services will confirm the operational priority during screening.

2. Your details

Official case records and decisions remain in English.
SMS and WhatsApp appear when enabled by the College.

3. Explain the matter

Attach receipts, screenshots, messages or other evidence that helps explain the matter.
Up to 10 files, maximum 15 MB each. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.

Track an existing ticket

Use the permanent reference and the email used to submit the matter.

What happens next?

  1. Student Support records the request for monitoring.
  2. The responsible unit receives the same reference and provides updates.
  3. You can add information, accept a resolution, reopen the matter or appeal.

Sensitive complaints use a restricted confidential pathway.
