UCC Academic Submission Portals v29

Developer passwordless portal preview
- Added a Portal Preview tab to the Developer/System Administrator portal.
- Developer can open Department Administration, Payroll or Auditor portals without entering the target user's password.
- Standard preview identities: Department Administrator, Department Officer, Department Viewer, Payroll Officer and Auditor.
- Developer can also preview an existing active administrator account with its exact role, department and section permissions.
- Added Preview shortcut to active administrator records.
- Preview session lasts 30 minutes and uses the existing secure HTTP-only administrator session cookie.
- Protected portals display a Developer Preview Mode banner with the preview identity and expiry time.
- Preview banner includes Return to Developer Portal and Exit Preview.
- Actions carried out during developer preview are explicitly attributed as Developer Preview actions in review/payroll audit fields.
- Added direct public-portal links in the preview tab for Project Work, Field Experience and Teaching Practice, Dissertation and Assessment Report submission portals.
