UCC CoDE Academic Submission Portal v24

FIELD EXPERIENCE AND TEACHING PRACTICE ONLY

1. Resources now contain six separate Excel template downloads:
   - Field Experience I & II
   - Field Experience III & IV
   - Field Experience V
   - Micro-Teaching
   - Macro-Teaching
   - Reflection
2. Public Field Experience and Teaching Practice portal now uses study-centre checkboxes and permits multiple study-centre selections.
3. Field Experience I & II remains one submission sheet, but approved outputs are split into individual Field Experience I and Field Experience II reports.
4. Field Experience III & IV remains one submission sheet, but approved outputs are split into individual Field Experience III and Field Experience IV reports.
5. The admin portal provides separate report controls for Field Experience I, II, III, IV, V, Micro-Teaching, Macro-Teaching and Reflection.
6. Each individual report has: Consolidated Scores, Programme by Centre ZIP, Master Scores and Register.
7. All report score rows are sorted by Registration number.
8. Programme by Centre uses the first three registration-number segments, e.g. BCF/CR/01/22/0002 -> BCF/CR/01.
9. These changes do not alter Undergraduate Project Work, Dissertation, or Assessment/Vetting workflows.
