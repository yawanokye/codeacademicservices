# v27 update: configurable claim forms, tabbed administration and incremental centre management

## Field Experience and Teaching Practice
- Added a Claim Form upload to the public Field Experience and Teaching Practice portal.
- The Claim Form defaults to **Optional** for a new v27 installation/update.
- Developer/System Administrator can classify the Field Experience Claim Form as **Optional** or **Compulsory** under **Portal Settings**.
- The server enforces the setting, so a compulsory Claim Form cannot be bypassed by the browser.
- Submitted Field Experience Claim Forms appear in the departmental submission-detail view.
- This requirement setting applies only to Field Experience and Teaching Practice. Other portals are unchanged.

## Cleaner administration tabs
- Department administration now has four top-level tabs: Undergraduate Project Work, Field Experience & Teaching Practice, Dissertation Submission, and Assessment/Vetting Reports.
- Project Work has separate Distance and Non-Residential sub-tabs.
- Field Experience has one tab per approved score-sheet category, showing one category at a time.
- Dissertation administration has Fresh, Revised, Final and Assignments sub-tabs.
- Assessment/Vetting has separate Assessment and Vetting sub-tabs.
- The Developer/System Administrator portal is also divided into Resources, Administrators, Study Centres and Portal Settings tabs.

## Incremental study-centre management
- Developer/System Administrator can add one public study centre to one or more departments without replacing existing lists.
- Every published centre has an individual Delete control within its department.
- The existing CSV replacement workflow remains available for intentional bulk replacement.
- The Study Centre Code Directory also supports adding/updating one centre code at a time and deleting individual centre-code records.
- Existing Excel/CSV directory replacement and restore functions remain available.

No new npm package or Render environment variable is required.
