CoDE Academic Services Portal v38.0.0
Deferment, resumption and registration requests

New service-request categories

1. Request for Deferment
   Responsible unit: Student Support Services Unit

2. Request for Resumption from Deferment
   Responsible unit: Student Support Services Unit

3. Request for Resumption from Rustication
   Responsible unit: Student Support Services Unit

4. Registration Challenge
   Responsible unit: Registration Officer Portal
   Student Support Services also receives the same permanent reference for monitoring and follow-up.

Registration Officer Portal

- Added as a distinct functional unit.
- Can be opened through the Developer Portal preview controls.
- Can be assigned to viewer, officer or administrator staff accounts.
- Receives registration challenges automatically.
- Supports assignment by institutional email, secure staff links, red/yellow/green status indicators, evidence, reassignment, audit history and downloadable registers.

Student guidance

- Added all four categories to the public Student Support form and Centre Coordinator assisted-submission form.
- Added frequently requested service links.
- Added category-specific evidence guidance.
- Added English, Twi and French knowledge-base entries.

- Routing checks confirm the three deferment or resumption requests go to Student Support Services and registration challenges go to the Registration Officer Portal while remaining visible to Student Support.
