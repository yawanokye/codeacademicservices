CoDE Academic Services Portal v41

STAFF ASSIGNMENT RELIABILITY

- Assignment failures are converted into plain, actionable messages inside both the Student Support and functional-unit portals.
- Invalid email, non-institutional email, suspended account, completed case, busy database, full storage and storage-permission failures now explain the corrective action.
- Unexpected assignment failures are caught at the server boundary and no longer expose raw response code to the portal user.
- First-time permanent account creation, account activation, account reuse and secure staff links remain unchanged.

FINAL DECISION AND COLOUR AUDIT

- New staff assignment: red.
- Secure assignment opened by the assigned staff member: yellow.
- Resolution checklist completed or final decision issued: green.
- Redirected or superseded assignment: blue in the former unit's historical record.
- Each change stores the date, officer, source and narrative.
- Recording a final decision completes every active assignment for the current responsible unit and synchronizes the green indicator across authorised displays.
- The colour indicator has a click-to-open panel containing its history and the recorded resolution or final-decision narrative.
- Student ticket tracking provides a click-to-open decision narrative without exposing internal-only data.

STUDENT SUPPORT REASSIGNMENT

- Student Support can redirect any complaint or request visible within its authorised queue.
- The reassignment starts from the actual current responsible unit, not Student Support's oversight registration.
- The new unit receives the same permanent reference, evidence and history.
- The former unit keeps a read-only blue redirected entry showing the destination, date, officer and reason.
- Student Support remains actively registered for oversight and follow-up.
- Prior final decisions are retained in decision history if a case is redirected for further action.

DOWNLOADABLE REGISTERS

- Student Support and functional-unit CSV/XLSX registers include redirection history.
- Registers include the final decision or resolution narrative, deciding officer, decision date and earlier decision history.
