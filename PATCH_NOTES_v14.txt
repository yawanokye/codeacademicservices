UCC Submission Portal v14 patch

Main change: Undergraduate Project Work verification gate.

- New and legacy project-work submissions default to Pending Verification unless explicitly reviewed.
- Only Approved project-work submissions are included in consolidated and master score outputs.
- Department project-work Administrators can Approve, Reject, Return for Correction, or reset to Pending.
- Project-work rows are colour coded: amber Pending, green Approved, red Rejected, blue Returned.
- Review detail shows supervisor/examiner identity, email, study centre, score rows, submitted files and audit metadata.
- Automated warning flags cover repeat supervisors, repeat supervisor/study-centre combinations, registration numbers already found in other Approved score sheets, high row counts, and future secure-link expiry/revocation metadata.
- Warning flags are advisory. Administrators may approve after review.
- Project Work Register now includes review status, review timestamp, reviewer and note.
- Public project-work confirmation states that the submission is Pending Verification.
- Optional environment variable: PROJECT_HIGH_ROW_WARNING=100.
