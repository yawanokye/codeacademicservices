UCC CoDE Academic Submission Portal v32

Central operations access

- Removed Payroll and Auditor links from the Department chooser and Department Administration header.
- Added a dedicated Payroll & Auditor tab in the Developer Portal.
- Developer operations access opens a shared session that can switch among all four departments.

Payroll and Auditor selection

- Added Department, Activity and Payroll Status selectors.
- Activity choices include Undergraduate Project Work, all six Field Experience and Teaching Practice options, Dissertation Assessment and Dissertation Vetting.
- Dissertation Assessment and Vetting claims require department approval before Payroll can see them.
- Auditor visibility remains restricted to claims marked Approved for Payment or Paid by Payroll.

Compact page layout

- Replaced the fourteen-column Payroll and Auditor tables with eight grouped columns.
- Grouped claimant, activity, quantities, reconciliation, claim preview, payment status and actions into readable compact cells.
- Prevented action buttons from collapsing into narrow vertical text.

Field Experience default

- Department of Education Programmes is now selected by default on the Field Experience and Teaching Practice public submission form.
- Submitters can still change the department when required.
