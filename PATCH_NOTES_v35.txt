CoDE Academic Services v35, Priority 3 service-quality improvements
===================================================================

Knowledge base and frequently requested services
------------------------------------------------
- Added a searchable Student Support Knowledge Base linked from the home page and support portal.
- Includes guided preparation for incomplete results, fees, transcripts, certificates, centre changes, centre transit, name changes, programme matters, assessments and confidential complaints.
- Frequently requested services open the complaint or request form with the correct type, category and assistance language already selected.
- Added clear answers on duplicate tickets, tracking, evidence requests, confidential handling and mobile alerts.

Student satisfaction survey
---------------------------
- Expanded the closure survey from one rating to five service-quality dimensions: overall satisfaction, ease of use, communication, timeliness and staff courtesy.
- Captures whether the matter was resolved, whether notifications were helpful and whether language assistance met the student's need.
- Any low score requires a short explanation so corrective action is possible.
- Survey results feed unit and centre dashboards and performance workbooks.

Optional SMS and WhatsApp notifications
---------------------------------------
- Students may opt into Email and SMS or Email and WhatsApp when the relevant channel is enabled.
- Mobile numbers are normalised and validated before the ticket is accepted.
- Mobile alerts contain only the permanent reference, current stage and secure tracking link. They do not expose complaint details.
- Delivery outcomes are retained in the ticket notification history.
- Email remains the primary service record.

Multilingual assistance
-----------------------
- Added English, Twi and French assistance in the public knowledge base.
- Students can record their preferred assistance language on a complaint or request.
- The support form shows translated evidence guidance where needed.
- SMS and WhatsApp alerts use the selected assistance language.
- Official case records and decisions remain in English.

Centre and unit service-performance reports
-------------------------------------------
- Every authenticated staff level has a protected report workspace based on existing permissions.
- Reports can be filtered by period, study centre, functional unit, matter type, status and category.
- Performance is summarised by study centre and functional unit, including caseload, complaints, service requests, open and resolved cases, overdue and at-risk cases, SLA compliance, survey response, satisfaction and resolution time.
- Downloadable Excel performance workbooks contain summary, centre and unit sheets.

Complaint and request registers at all levels
---------------------------------------------
- Student Support can download the filtered register as Excel or CSV.
- Functional units, coordinators, regional administrators, monitoring officers and leadership can download Excel or CSV registers for only the cases they are permitted to see.
- Sensitive complaints remain limited to the confidential handler and authorised Provost oversight.
- Registers include workflow, SLA, student contact, centre, unit, survey and notification fields.
- Spreadsheet formula-injection protection is applied to exported text.

Optional production settings
----------------------------
SUPPORT_SMS_ENABLED=false
SUPPORT_WHATSAPP_ENABLED=false
TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID=
TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=
TWILIO_SMS_FROM=
TWILIO_WHATSAPP_FROM=

Set PUBLIC_BASE_URL so mobile and email alerts contain a valid secure tracking link. Keep the mobile switches false until the approved sender and production credentials have been tested.
