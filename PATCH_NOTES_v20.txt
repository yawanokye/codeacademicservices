New behaviour:
1. Return for Correction requires a non-empty reason.
2. The reason is emailed to the Project Work or Field Experience supervisor/examiner via Gmail API.
3. The email includes submission reference, study centre, correction reason and a link back to the appropriate public submission portal.
4. Returned submissions show Sent/Failed/Pending correction-email status.
5. Existing returned submissions can use Resend Correction Email without resetting their review status.
6. Email failure does not reverse the Returned for Correction status.
7. Resend Correction Email prompts for the recipient address, allowing correction of a mistyped submission email while preserving the original record.
