UCC Academic Submission Portals v23

Field Experience and Teaching Practice update

1. Public portal renamed to "Field Experience and Teaching Practice".
2. One assessment category is submitted per submission:
   - Field Experience I & II
   - Field Experience III & IV
   - Field Experience V
   - Micro-Teaching
   - Macro-Teaching
   - Reflection
3. The Field Experience public portal now validates the selected assessment against the correct Excel columns.
4. The approved six-sheet Excel workbook is included as a built-in Field Experience and Teaching Practice resource.
5. The old Project Work score-sheet sample is no longer shown as a Field Experience resource.
6. Admin Field Experience records are grouped into the same six assessment categories.
7. Admin review displays the extracted student rows using the correct score columns for the selected assessment.
8. Consolidated Field/Teaching export contains a separate worksheet for each assessment category. Approved records only are included and rows are sorted by registration number.
9. Master Field/Teaching export contains the same assessment-specific worksheets plus the Field/Teaching register.
10. The Field/Teaching register now includes Assessment Type and uses assessment-specific score-row counts.
11. Existing pre-v23 Field Experience records remain visible under Previous / Unclassified Field Experience and are preserved in legacy exports where applicable.
12. Other portals and their workflows are unchanged.

No new environment variables or npm dependencies are required.
