Central Payroll and Auditor Access

Payroll and Auditor access is managed here, separately from Department Administration. Select the starting department, then open the required portal. The portal itself allows authorised switching between departments and activities.

Developer operations sessions last 30 minutes. Payroll actions are live and recorded as Developer Preview actions. The Auditor Portal remains read only.

Portal Preview / Passwordless Developer Login

Open a department, Payroll or Auditor portal without entering that user's password. Use a standard preview role or reproduce the permissions of an existing active administrator account.

Developer preview sessions last 30 minutes. The target portal displays a visible preview banner. If you use an Administrator or Officer profile, any action you perform is a real action and is recorded as a Developer Preview action.

Only active accounts assigned to the selected department are shown. No password is read or exposed.
